Overall Accuracy

--%
Measures how often the model is correct overall. A high accuracy means it generally makes the right call on whether a leaf is diseased or healthy.

Binary Precision

--%
When the model sounds the alarm (predicts disease), how often is it actually a disease? High precision means fewer false alarms.

Binary Recall

--%
Out of all truly diseased leaves, how many did the model successfully catch? High recall means fewer missed diseases.

False Positive Rate

--%
The percentage of perfectly healthy leaves that were incorrectly flagged as diseased (false alarms).
